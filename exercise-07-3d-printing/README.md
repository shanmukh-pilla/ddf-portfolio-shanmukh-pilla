# Exercise 07: Onshape CAD Self-Study

## Overview

This exercise focused on learning the fundamentals of Computer-Aided Design (CAD) using the Onshape cloud-based CAD platform. The objective was to complete the required self-study training modules and become familiar with parametric modelling, sketch creation, and part design workflows commonly used in digital fabrication and engineering design.

The exercise required completion of three training courses provided by Onshape:

- Introduction to Parametric Feature-Based CAD
- Introduction to Sketching
- Introduction to Part Studios

---

## Course 1 – Introduction to Parametric Feature-Based CAD

This course introduced the basic principles of parametric and feature-based modelling. I learned how CAD models are built using editable dimensions, constraints, and feature histories, allowing designs to be modified efficiently without rebuilding them from scratch.

---

## Course 2 – Introduction to Sketching

This course focused on creating sketches that serve as the foundation for 3D models. I learned how to use sketching tools, apply dimensions and constraints, and create fully defined sketches that maintain design accuracy and consistency.

---

## Course 3 – Introduction to Part Studios

This course introduced the Part Studio environment within Onshape and demonstrated how sketches are transformed into three-dimensional parts. I learned how modelling tools such as Extrude, Revolve, Fillet, and Chamfer are used to create and refine CAD models.

---

## Course Completion

The required Onshape training modules were completed successfully.

## Reflection

This exercise provided a useful introduction to professional CAD software and digital design workflows. Through the Onshape training modules, I gained a better understanding of parametric modelling, sketch-based design, and 3D part creation.

The training helped me understand how digital models are developed before fabrication and manufacturing. These skills will be valuable for future Digital Design and Fabrication projects involving laser cutting, CNC machining, and 3D printing.
